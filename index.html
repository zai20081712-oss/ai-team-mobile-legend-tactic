Gold Laner & Roamer Synergy

The roamer should help secure the lithowanderer at 0:45, then assist the mid lane before rotating back to protect the gold laner from dive attempts.

Jungling

Efficient Retribution Pathing

Start on the side opposite to the enemy jungler's expected path to avoid early skirmishes, or secure level 4 for first turtle contest.

  • ✓ Buff -> Little minions -> Buff

Objectives

Turtle & Lord Control

Turtle spawns at 2:00. Clear adjacent minion waves 20 seconds prior to turtle spawn so your team has priority to initiate or zone.

  • ✓ Push minion waves before fight
  • ✓ Zone enemy jungler out of pit
  • ✓ Lord dynamic scaling after min 12

Hero Counter Matrix

Know who to pick or ban against high-priority meta threats.

Threat: Fanny (Assassin)

Hard Counters: Khufra, Minsitthar, Saber

Khufra's bouncing ball stops cable dashes instantly. Minsitthar's ultimate locks down mobility skills.

Threat: Wanwan (Marksman)

Hard Counters: Khaleed, Phoveus, Twilight Armor item

Phoveus triggers skills whenever agile heroes dash. Keep your back against walls to block weakness triggers.

Threat: Estes (Support)

Hard Counters: Necklace of Durance, Dominance Ice, Sea Halberd

Stack anti-heal items across multiple roles to completely shut down his massive team healing.
